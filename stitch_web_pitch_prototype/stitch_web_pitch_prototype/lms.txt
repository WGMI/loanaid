Full Explanation

Reaching the Unbanked: A Field-to-Core Lending Platform
The problem: Malawi's rural population — smallholder farmers, small traders, first-time borrowers — has no credit history. Traditional collateral-based lending shuts them out, and sending loan officers to remote areas with paper forms is slow, error-prone, and impossible to audit or scale.

The solution: An end-to-end digital lending platform built specifically for that reality, in production for Centenary Bank Malawi.

How it works
In the field, a loan officer carries an Android app that works with zero connectivity. They walk to a farm, capture the applicant's identity, household, and business data through a guided step-by-step wizard, take geotagged photos, record a GPS-verified location, and complete a signature — all stored securely on the device. No signal required. When the officer's phone reconnects — back at a trading center, in town, wherever — the application syncs automatically in the background.

At the bank, that submission hits a credit-scoring engine that replaces the one thing rural borrowers don't have: a credit history. Instead of requiring collateral or a bureau file, it scores the applicant on alternative data — household stability, business viability, verified location, behavioral signals — using a configurable, auditable scorecard. The decision comes back in seconds: auto-approve, decline, or route to a human for manual review.

One platform, one system of record. The same core banking platform that runs this loan product also runs the bank's entire mobile money and digital channel business — bill payments, mobile wallets, merchant payments, card switching, USSD banking, SMS banking, and full back-office administration with maker-checker controls and audit trails. This isn't a bolted-on pilot; it's a module in a production banking system already processing real transaction volume.

Why this matters at the C-suite level
New revenue the bank cannot currently capture. Every unbanked household with viable income is a customer competitors also can't reach with traditional underwriting. This is how you originate that loan volume without adding branches.

Risk configured, not hard-coded. The scoring rules — what data matters, how it's weighted, where the approval cutoffs sit — are configuration, not code. Credit policy can be tuned by product and by market condition without an engineering release cycle or app store deployment. That's a materially faster response to portfolio performance than a typical core-banking change request.

Built for the infrastructure that actually exists in rural Malawi. Offline-first isn't a feature checkbox — it's the only way this works, since connectivity is unreliable exactly where the target customers live. The alternative is officers with paper forms and manual data entry, which is what your competitors who don't have this are running.

Auditability and control from day one. Every score, every approval, every officer action is logged. GPS and photo evidence with EXIF metadata make fraud materially harder. Encrypted local storage plus device-level identity protects both customer data and the bank's compliance posture.

Full loan lifecycle in one place — application, scoring, decision, and it runs alongside the bank's existing mobile money, collections, bill payment, and card infrastructure — this is not a point solution that will need to be re-integrated later. It's already part of the core stack.
